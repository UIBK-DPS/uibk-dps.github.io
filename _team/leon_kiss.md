Leon is a Computer Science student at the University of Innsbruck in Austria, where he is currently pursuing his MSc and working as a student assistant for the Distributed and Parallel Systems research group. He explores topics such as edge-cloud computing, decentralized container scheduling, serverless computing, peer-to-peer networks, and energy-efficient resource allocation, with a current emphasis on designing multi-objective, market-based scheduling architectures for heterogeneous clusters.
